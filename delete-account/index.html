Delete your Card Arise data

Last updated: 10 November 2026 · Contact: estimatedly@gmail.com

You can delete your Card Arise account and saved progress from inside the App, or by emailing us. This page is the web path required for apps that offer accounts.

Delete in the App (recommended)

  1. Open Card Arise on your device.
  2. Open Settings (gear icon) or Account.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. If you are signed in, re-enter your password and confirm. If you are playing as a guest, confirm to clear the trial save on this device.

This permanently deletes:

  • Your local account and game save on that device
  • Your cloud save and account record on our servers (when the account was linked to the cloud)
  • Your Live Board record, if one was published
  • The Card Arise Play Games snapshot, if you are signed in to Play Games

Delete by email

If you cannot open the App, email estimatedly@gmail.com from a message that identifies your in-game display name and, if you still have it, your account ID (shown on the Account screen). We will delete the matching cloud account and save. We may ask a short question to confirm it is your account. We aim to complete requests within 14 days.

What uninstalling does

Uninstalling Card Arise removes data stored on that device only. It does not delete a cloud save. Use in-app deletion or email if you also want the cloud copy removed.

Advertising data

Ads are served by Google AdMob. Advertising identifiers and ad-interaction data held by Google are handled under Google’s Privacy Policy. You can reset or opt out of the advertising ID in your Android settings.
