Card Arise — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026 · Contact: estimatedly@gmail.com

Card Arise (“the App”) is a single-player fantasy card battle game for Android, published at https://cardarise.com. This policy describes the information the App stores or sends when you play the current release.

1. Information stored on your device

Game progress, settings, your collection, and (if you create one) a local account are stored on your device. A local account uses a unique display name and a password. The password is stored only as a salted hash on the device — never as plain text.

If you play as a guest / trial player, only a local save is kept, with no account name unless you later create one.

2. Optional cloud save

If you create or link an account, the App may store a cloud copy of your save so you can restore progress after an update or on a new phone. That copy is hosted on our cloud save provider and includes:

  • Your user identifier and display name (username)
  • A synthetic sign-in address derived from your display name (not a personal email you type into the App)
  • Your game save (progress, collection, settings)
  • If you publish to the Live Board, your display name and basic play statistics (level, wins, tower, points)

This cloud data is used only to provide the App (account, save restore, and the optional Live Board). We do not sell it.

3. Optional Google Play Games

If you later sign in with Google Play Games (when that option is configured), Google may process your Play Games player ID and a cloud save snapshot under Google’s terms. In the current release this sign-in is not required, and play continues normally without it.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free to play and contains optional ads:

  • Optional rewarded ads only, shown only when you choose an offer in the Shop’s “Free Rewards” section. There are no banner ads and no ads that appear on their own.

Ads are served by Google AdMob. Google may collect and process your device advertising ID, IP address (which may be used to infer approximate location), device and app information, and ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, and reward events) for ad delivery, measurement, frequency capping, analytics, and fraud prevention. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can skip a rewarded ad before it starts. You can reset or opt out of the advertising ID in your Android settings. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent (via Google’s UMP consent form) before personalized ads are requested.

5. Purchases

The current release of Card Arise has no real-money in-app purchases. Gems and other in-game currency are earned in play or by watching optional rewarded ads. They cannot be bought with real money in the App.

6. Children

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. How long we keep data, and how you can delete it

Device data stays on your phone until you delete the account or the guest save, or uninstall the App. Cloud save and Live Board records are kept so you can restore or publish progress, and are removed when you delete your account in the App or by asking us to delete them.

You can delete your account and save data in the App: open Settings or Account, tap Delete account, and confirm (re-enter your password if you have one). This removes the local account and save, requests deletion of the cloud save, and removes a Play Games snapshot if you are signed in there. Guest players can clear the trial save the same way.

You can also follow the steps at https://cardarise.com/delete-account/ or email estimatedly@gmail.com. Uninstalling the App removes data stored on that device only; it does not by itself delete a cloud save.

8. Sharing

We share advertising-related data with Google AdMob as described above. Cloud save and account data are processed by our service provider on our behalf, to run the App. We do not sell your information. We may disclose information if required by law.

9. Security and international processing

Passwords are hashed. Cloud traffic uses HTTPS. Our service providers may process data on servers outside your country.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the App changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Questions or deletion requests: estimatedly@gmail.com.
